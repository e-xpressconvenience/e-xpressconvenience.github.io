How does ordering work?

Add your essentials, confirm where they should go and open your quote request in WhatsApp. Our team confirms stock, the total and delivery before you pay.

Will I know the delivery fee before I commit?

Yes. We include the delivery arrangement and charge in your confirmed quote. This website does not collect payment or reserve stock.

Can I order for someone else?

Yes. Enter their delivery address, recipient name and contact number. Use their destination rather than your current location.
