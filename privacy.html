YOUR LOCATION, YOUR CHOICE

A little clarity
before you share.

Location is optional

We request your device location only when you tap “Use my current location”. If you decline, enter an address or choose a map pin instead. We do not continuously track customers in the background.

Check the delivery destination

GPS can be inaccurate. Confirm the pin and add a landmark or house number. When ordering for someone else, use their address and contact details with their permission.

What stays on this device

Your basket is saved in this browser. Delivery details are kept for this tab’s session. Only selecting “Remember delivery details on this device” stores them between visits. Avoid this on shared devices. You can clear the details in checkout.

What is shared

Opening the quote in WhatsApp includes the basket, recipient details, destination and payment preference in a draft for you to review and send. WhatsApp’s own terms apply. When arranging delivery, Xpress needs to share delivery details with authorised operations staff and the assigned rider.

Optional map

Opening the map loads Leaflet from jsDelivr and tiles from OpenStreetMap. Those providers receive normal connection information, such as your IP address and the map area requested. You can use the address fields without opening the map.

Accounts and delivery operations

Account functions use Supabase. The delivery operations area is separate from this quote checkout. A quote request is not a paid order or a stock reservation.

Questions or removal requests

Contact expressconvenience2.4.7@gmail.com or +266 6354 0048 about information you have shared with Xpress.

Camera and photo search

Camera access starts only when you choose Scan a barcode. You can stop it at any time. Photos and camera frames are processed on your device; Xpress does not upload or store them. The label reader downloads software and language files from jsDelivr, which receives normal connection information but not your photo. Search terms appear in the search page address. Photos are cleared when you reset or leave the scanner. You decide whether to send a reference photo separately in WhatsApp.
