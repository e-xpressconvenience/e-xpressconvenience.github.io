Who we are

An online-first convenience business.

Express Convenience is an e-commerce and doorstep delivery business focused on making everyday shopping easier. Our platform is being developed so customers can explore products, place orders and arrange delivery from wherever they are.

We are building the experience in stages: a clear product catalogue, a simple ordering process, practical digital payment choices and dependable communication from enquiry to delivery.

Our mission

Make everyday shopping more convenient through a simple e-commerce platform, flexible payment options and reliable doorstep delivery.

Our direction

A service people can rely on.

Our vision

To develop Express Convenience into a trusted, accessible and technology-enabled e-commerce and delivery service.

Our operating model

Our customer-facing business is online and delivery based. Product fulfilment and delivery are coordinated behind the scenes as the platform grows.

The customer experience

From browsing to delivery.

The full online journey is in development. The intended experience will make it easy to browse, select, check out, pay, and follow an order through preparation and delivery.

01 / Browse

Find products

Explore available items and choose what you need.

02 / Checkout

Confirm details

Provide order and delivery information in a simple flow.

03 / Payment

Choose a method

Use a supported option, including a planned wallet-transfer flow.

04 / Verification

Confirm the order

Payment and order details are checked before processing.

05 / Prepare

Get it ready

Products are prepared for dispatch.

06 / Deliver

To your doorstep

The order is delivered to the agreed location.

What works today: This website currently offers a product enquiry basket and WhatsApp contact. A full priced catalogue, integrated payment checkout and online order tracking are planned features, not live transactions.
